The Solution

Paid subscription communities
inside Blossom.

Tiered Subscriptions Content Feed Discussion Forums FAQ Zone Creator Dashboard Earnings Tracking
04
How It Works

Two sides. One platform.

Subscriber

Discovery & Preview

Browse by category, preview communities, compare tier benefits before subscribing.

Tier-Gated Content

Trade highlights, analysis posts, YouTube deep-links — unlocked by subscription level.

Discussion Forums

Async threads, not chat noise. Creator replies highlighted. No Discord burnout.

FAQ Zone

Structured Q&A with verified creator answers.

Unique to Blossom — no competitor has this
Creator

Community Setup

Brand your hub with logo, banner, description. Define 1–4 subscription tiers.

Content Publishing

Compose posts, assign collections, set tier gates. Text, trades, and YouTube formats.

Permissions Matrix

Control exactly which tiers access which sections — posts, forums, FAQ, videos.

Earnings Dashboard

Revenue charts, subscriber counts, transparent 10% fee breakdown. Full visibility.

05
The Flywheel

Every user compounds the whole business.

More Users → More 1P Data → Better Ad Resolution → More Ad Spend

Hubs keeps users inside Blossom instead of losing them to Discord. Every subscriber generates proprietary behavioral signals — content preferences, engagement patterns, investment interests — that make our ad placements more targeted, more valuable, and more attractive to advertisers.

06
Revenue Model

Three revenue streams. One feature.

10% Platform Fee Transparent 10% cut on every subscriber payment. Creators see it clearly in their earnings dashboard.
Ads New Placements Banner, inline card, and pill ad formats across discovery, feeds, and category pages. 1P data makes every impression more valuable.
PRO+ Bundle Upsell Cross-sell Blossom PRO to engaged community subscribers. Deeper analytics, more reasons to stay.
